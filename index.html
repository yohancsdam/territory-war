Territory War

Turn-based stickman battle for two players on the same device.

Red Team
-- No battle
Blue Team
    How to play
    • Each team has 6 soldiers and plays one of them per turn, alternating between teams. The team that eliminates every enemy soldier wins.
    • A soldier is eliminated when their health runs out or when they are knocked off the screen (sides or bottom). Falling doesn't hurt.
    • Move: A and D or ←/→. Jump: W. Aim: mouse (or ↑/↓). Switch weapon: keys 1 to 6 or the buttons.
    • Fire: pistol, shotgun and sniper fire on click or Space. Bazooka, grenade and dynamite charge while you hold click or Space and launch on release. Esc cancels the charge.
    • You can only fire once per turn and you have 60 seconds. If time runs out, you lose the turn. There is no wind.
    • Friendly fire is on: your shots and explosions also hurt your own team.
    • On a touch screen, drag on the battlefield to aim and use the buttons to move.
